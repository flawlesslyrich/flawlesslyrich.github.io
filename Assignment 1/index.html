🗡️ SHADOWHOLD: DEPTHS 🛡️

A text-based dungeon crawler where you explore cursed ruins, battle foul creatures, and grow stronger.
▶ Use the console-like menu to survive the depths. Every choice matters.
⚙️ GAME FEATURES
• 🧭 Explore – random enemy encounters & loot
• 📊 View Stats – health, attack, level, gold, XP
• 🎒 Inventory – manage potions, consumables
• 🍖 Rest – recover HP (costs gold)
• 💀 Permadeath warning – defeat resets your progress!
🔁 Menu driven with full validation & game loop
⚔️ prompt() • alert() • confirm() only | Modular JS (ES6 modules)
